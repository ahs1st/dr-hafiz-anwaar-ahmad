About the Doctor

Compassionate, evidence-based cardiac care

Dr. Hafiz Anwaar Ahmad Sangra is a well-known, trusted and highly capable Consultant Cardiologist in Faisalabad. He has served at the Faisalabad Institute of Cardiology (FIC) and various District Headquarter hospitals, giving him exceptional command of complex cardiac diagnosis, modern echocardiography, angiography and emergency cardiac care.

He treats every patient with deep compassion, patience and sincerity — explaining each condition and its treatment in simple words, avoiding unnecessary expensive tests, and recommending the most economical yet best course of care in line with modern guidelines.

Qualifications

Clinical Specialties & Procedures

Complete heart & blood pressure care under one roof

Diagnosis and treatment of all cardiac conditions as per the clinical record and standing banner.

Cardiac Diagnostics

  • ECGElectrical activity & rhythm of the heart
  • EchocardiographyColour Doppler echo — chambers & valve function
  • ETT (Exercise Tolerance Test)Stress test of the heart & arteries
  • Holter Monitoring24-hour continuous rhythm recording
  • Calcium ScoringMeasures calcium build-up & blockage risk

Interventional Cardiology

  • AngiographyX-ray study of blood flow & blockages
  • Angioplasty / StentingOpening blocked arteries with balloon & stents

Critical Conditions

  • Heart Attack / MIEmergency, life-saving treatment
  • Angina PectorisChest pain from narrowed arteries
  • Heart FailureCardiomyopathy & weakened muscle care
  • Pulmonary EdemaFluid in the lungs from heart weakness
  • Valvular DiseaseStenosis or regurgitation of valves
  • Congenital DefectsBirth defects & holes in children and adults

General Cardiac Symptoms

  • HypertensionDefinitive control of high blood pressure
  • DyslipidemiaCholesterol & triglycerides management
  • Arrhythmia / PalpitationsIrregular or fast heartbeat care
  • Dyspnea / Shortness of BreathDetailed evaluation of breathing difficulty
  • Syncope / DizzinessFainting & reduced brain blood supply
  • Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT)Clots in the leg veins — care & prevention
